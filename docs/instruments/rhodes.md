# Rhodes — tine electric piano (`rhodes / mk1`)

Target: Rhodes MK8. Reference: **jRhodes3d** — a 1977 Rhodes Mark I
Stage 73 sampled by Jeffrey Learman, DI from the harp connector, CC
BY-NC 4.0 (samples; analysis-only use, attribution here and in
SOURCES.md). No license-clean MK8 multisample exists (only the
proprietary Rhodes V8 plugin), so this is a documented reference
substitution like the koto/đàn tranh: the MK8 shares the Mark I's
tine + tonebar + electromagnetic pickup physics; re-calibration on a
licensed MK8 set is the upgrade path. 15 pitches F1–C7 × up to 5
velocity layers, 44.1 kHz, unlooped decays to 25 s, no room, no hum —
the bank's cleanest reference chain (the author's fixed EQ voicing is
absorbed into the tables like any chain response).

## Sound model

Generic modal engine (`config` table), no engine feature additions:

- **Partials are exact harmonics** (n-series, B = 0). Physics: after
  ~10 ms the tine is a pure decaying sinusoid; the harmonic series is
  generated by the pickup's memoryless nonlinearity (flux ≈
  a + k/(x+gap)²), so partial n sits at exactly n·f0 and its level
  scales ≈ (swing)ⁿ — which is why per-velocity-layer static fits are
  structurally right and why the analysis snaps `find_partials`' free
  inharmonic fit onto a forced-harmonic grid.
- Per-layer double-exponential amplitude fits per harmonic (≤24), from
  complex-demodulation envelopes with SNR gating, demod-settle
  exclusion, and the woodblock's t=0 amplitude caps (see DEVLOG for
  the four fitting schemes that lost).
- Hammer/key thunk in the per-band thump (`thump_tau_bands`); the DI
  chain has no room, and preamp hiss below −45 dB of the loudest thump
  band is gated out of the bed.
- Dampers: `release_fade_s` 0.15, all keys.
- Velocity map (sfz groups): v1..v5 → 24/60/84/104/120.

## Results (2026-07-11)

- Composite (65 cells, EP-specific weights — harmonic balance,
  sustain decay, attack): **0.609 mean / 0.509 median**, seed spread
  ±0.006. Perturbed-self null 0.280 ± 0.197 (register-dependent:
  bass 0.5–0.8, treble 0.06–0.2); mid/treble cells sit at the null.
- Rust port: note_params ≤ 1.9e-15; rust eval 0.638 = inside the
  Python seed null. Shipping the table exposed and fixed a latent
  engine bug: B = 0 tables NaN'd the log-domain B interpolation
  between keys (panic in the salience sort) — `interp.rs` now mirrors
  the Python floor/clamp, `voice.rs` sorts with `total_cmp`.
- Quality: full 24 partials 0.638 → p16 0.723 → p8 0.842. Keep ≥16.

## Known limitations

- **Loud-bass bloom**: hard-struck low notes' fundamentals *rise* for
  seconds (pickup harmonic redistribution) — unrepresentable by
  nonnegative double-exponentials; those ~8 cells plateau at 1.2–1.4.
  Engine-extension candidate (signed fast component) costed and
  deferred; audible as a smooth sustain instead of a swelling growl.
- Per-take f0 drift in the reference (±15 c on F1) is collapsed to one
  f0 per key.
- The jRhodes EQ voicing ("bell + bark emphasized") is part of the
  model's identity; a flat-harp or MK8 voicing needs re-calibration.
